# RC0 Explicit Non-Claims

This experiment does not establish:

- correctness or completeness of CAL semantics;
- truth of the synthetic domain-shaped propositions;
- current CAL reachability of every consumer-valid Contract C case;
- correctness of source provenance or evidence sufficiency outside the exact contracted inputs;
- that Decision Engine v1.0.0 should support every desired downstream workflow decision;
- that the known Contract-B internal-reference aperture is newly discovered;
- that a generic raw authority schema, policy registry, plugin system, DSL, or caller-controlled evaluator is safe or required;
- that `knowledge.add_verified_tag@1` means note/document/SOP approval;
- that `knowledge.cite_as_evidence@1` establishes source truth, corpus completeness, or publication suitability;
- that a Contract D CLEAR is Authorization;
- operational actor authority, delegation, execution, mutation, task dispatch, or outcome verification;
- readiness for v1.0.1 or any later release;
- permission to merge this research branch.

The experiment may identify capability gaps or candidate successor questions. Any maintained change requires a separate bounded experiment and promotion/release decision.

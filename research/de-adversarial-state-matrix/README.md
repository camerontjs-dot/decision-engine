## Primary question

Can CAL naturally produce a valid mixed warranted support + warranted refutation state for one proposition, and does maintained Decision Engine fail closed without flattening that conflict into either support or contradiction?

Both must pass the frozen CAL measurement, atom, warrant, proposition-binding, and categorical-relation machinery. Contract C must be constructed and validated by CAL, not edited after production.

## Falsifiers

- mixed warranted evidence becomes `supported` or `contradicted` rather than safe abstention;
- Contract C validation fails after CAL's own projection;
- either maintained policy CLEARs the mixed/not-checkable proposition or either contribution;
- any binding, replay, policy, authority, or semantic-invalid mutation reaches CLEAR;
- maintained `src/**` or `scripts/**` must change for the expected safe behavior.
